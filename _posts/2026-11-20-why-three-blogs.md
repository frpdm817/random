Most people who start writing settle on one blog. I started three at the same time.

That sounds like too much, so here's why I did it.

## One person, three audiences

I work in IT. I'm also a new dad. And, like everyone, I have a pile of thoughts, stories and opinions that have nothing to do with either of those.

When I imagined putting all of it in one place, it didn't work. Someone who came for a post on software architecture doesn't need my notes on night feeds. A tired parent looking for "am I doing this right?" doesn't care about my deployment pipeline. And neither of them signed up for my opinion on how to load a dishwasher.

So I gave each one its own home:

- **[Engineering](https://engineering.fabiopereira.name)** is for software, systems and the craft of building things.
- **[Fatherhood](https://fatherhood.fabiopereira.name)** is for stories and lessons from being a dad, starting with [the day I found out I wasn't as ready as I thought](https://fatherhood.fabiopereira.name/2026/11/20/and-i-thought-i-was-ready/).
- **Random**, the one you're reading, is for everything else.

You can see all three together at [blog.fabiopereira.name](https://blog.fabiopereira.name).

## So what is "random"?

Honestly? Whatever doesn't fit in the other two.

It could be a trip, a book, a strong opinion about something that doesn't matter, a small thing that made my week better, or a story I tell at dinner that's too off-topic for anywhere else. If it doesn't belong in a blog about software or a blog about being a dad, it goes here.

No schedule, no theme, no promises.

## Why three languages too

You may have noticed the language switcher at the top. Every blog is in English, Spanish and Portuguese.

These are the languages I live in. Some of the people I'd most like to read this would rather do it in Spanish or Portuguese than in English, and I didn't want to leave them out. It's more work, but it means my family and friends can read along in the language they're most at home in.

## Come along

If you're here for one blog, the other two are a click away. And if you're here for this one: welcome to the drawer where everything else goes. Let's see what ends up in it.
